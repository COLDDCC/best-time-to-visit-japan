## Tokyo November weather at a glance

| JMA Tokyo station, 1991–2020 | November average | Use it for |
|---|---:|---|
| Daily high | 16.7°C | Choosing removable daytime layers |
| Daily low | 8.8°C | Planning a warmer morning or evening layer |
| Monthly precipitation | 96.3 mm | Comparing seasons, not predicting wet days |
| October precipitation, for comparison | 234.8 mm | Seeing why November is relatively drier |
| December precipitation, for comparison | 57.9 mm | Weighing drier averages against colder weather |

Source: [Japan Meteorological Agency climate normals](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/en/normal/normal.html). These are long-term station averages, not a forecast. Precipitation totals cannot tell you whether a particular week will stay dry.

## What to wear in Tokyo in November

| Your plan | Start with | Carry or check |
|---|---|---|
| Full day of city walking | Long sleeves, trousers, sweater, jacket | A bag for the layer you remove |
| Gardens or temple grounds | The same layers, shoes with grip | Compact umbrella for exposed paths |
| Evening illuminations | Warm layer and jacket | Scarf if you feel cold easily; confirm event dates |
| Rainy afternoon | Rain-tolerant walking shoes | Indoor backup and compact umbrella |
| Higher-elevation day trip | Clothing for that destination | Local forecast, access and route conditions |

Use the [November packing list](/blog/what-to-wear-in-japan-in-november/) for a shoe comparison and regional extensions. Broken-in shoes are a practical starting point for city walks; choose winter footwear according to snow and ice conditions on the actual route.

## Early or late November?

[JNTO's November guide](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/november/) describes increasingly chilly mornings and evenings, with autumn color varying by place. Use a monthly average to shortlist clothing, then check daily highs and lows near departure. Do not treat an early-November afternoon and a late-November evening as the same packing problem.

For trips continuing into winter, compare [late November and early December](/blog/japan-weather-late-november-early-december/).
