## Tokyo December weather at a glance

| JMA Tokyo station, 1991–2020 | December average | November, for comparison |
|---|---:|---:|
| Daily high | **12.0°C** | 16.7°C |
| Daily low | **3.8°C** | 8.8°C |
| Monthly precipitation | **57.9 mm** | 96.3 mm |
| Sunshine | **174.4 hours** | 149.8 hours |

Source: [Japan Meteorological Agency climate normals](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/en/normal/normal.html). These are long-term station averages, not a forecast for your dates.

**In short:** December in Tokyo is roughly 5°C colder than November, but drier and sunnier. Expect crisp, clear days and cold mornings and evenings rather than frequent rain.

## How cold is Tokyo in December?

An average high of 12.0°C is jacket-and-sweater weather for most visitors in the afternoon. The 3.8°C average low matters more if you start early, or stay out for evening illuminations. Wind along the waterfront and on open observation decks makes it feel colder still.

Compared with other cities, Tokyo is close to Osaka (12.3°C / 5.3°C), much milder than Sapporo (2.0°C / −4.0°C) and far colder than Naha in Okinawa (21.5°C / 16.8°C). See the [December weather guide](/month/december/) for six cities side by side.

## Does it snow in Tokyo in December?

Plan as though it will not. Snow is a realistic planning factor in Hokkaido, the Sea of Japan side and mountain areas, not in central Tokyo in December. If you want snow, add a northern or mountain destination and check its conditions separately. Our [December snow guide](/blog/does-it-snow-in-japan-in-december/) compares where snow is more likely.

## What to wear in Tokyo in December

| Your plan | Start with | Carry or check |
|---|---|---|
| Daytime city walking | Warm coat, sweater or fleece, long trousers | A bag for the layer you remove indoors |
| Early morning markets or temples | Add a scarf and a warmer base layer | Gloves if you feel the cold easily |
| Evening illuminations | Coat, scarf, gloves and a hat | Confirm the event's operating dates |
| Museums, shopping and trains | Layers you can take off | Heated interiors can feel warm in a full coat |
| Day trip to the mountains or snow | Clothing for that destination | Local forecast, snow and road or rail conditions |

## Early, mid or late December?

| When | What changes |
|---|---|
| Early December | Days are still relatively mild; some late autumn color may remain in places |
| Mid-December | Colder evenings; a good window for winter illuminations |
| Late December | Christmas events, then the year-end holiday period, when some businesses close |

[JNTO's December guide](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/december/) describes the New Year period as busy, with some businesses closing for a few days around December 29 to January 3. Schedules vary by venue, so check every must-see stop. Read [Christmas & New Year in Japan](/blog/christmas-new-year-japan/) if your dates cross the holidays.
