## Christmas or New Year: choose by your priority

| Your priority | Starting choice | Before booking |
|---|---|---|
| Winter lights and city sightseeing | December, with dates chosen around the events you want | Check each event's actual operating dates |
| New Year's Eve or first shrine visit of the year | Late December into early January | Check shrine arrangements and return transport |
| A flexible museum and restaurant itinerary | Dates outside a venue's holiday closure | Confirm individual opening calendars |
| Skiing or a snow-country stay | Choose a resort by its current snow and operating report | Confirm open terrain, transport and cancellation terms |
| Lower-friction long-distance travel | Compare dates outside the year-end holiday rush | Check fares and availability for your exact route |

[JNTO's December guide](https://www.japan.travel/en/guide/december/) describes the New Year period as busy and expensive, with some businesses closing for a few days. It gives December 29 to January 3 as a typical holiday window; individual schedules vary. This page is a planning guide, not a confirmed event calendar for 2026–27.

## Before Christmas: plan around the lights you want

Winter illuminations are a reason to consider a December city break. Some begin in November; dates differ by event. Choose the specific display first, then check admission, opening evenings and transport on the organizer's website. Do not assume that every display ends on Christmas Day.

Use the [December city weather table](/month/december/) and [December packing checklist](/blog/what-to-wear-in-japan-in-december/) for warmer evening layers.

## December 29 to early January: make a closure plan

| Check | What to record |
|---|---|
| Must-see attraction | Its exact open dates and last admission |
| Restaurant | Holiday schedule and reservation confirmation |
| Intercity transport | Available departures and reservation requirements |
| Local return journey | Last service or confirmed holiday timetable |
| Snow-country extension | Weather, open terrain and access report |

Keep one confirmed alternative for each day. A business closing does not mean an entire city closes, but it can change a carefully timed itinerary.

## Does Christmas mean snow in Tokyo?

A Tokyo or Osaka trip should not depend on snowfall. Northern and mountain destinations offer a different winter experience, but early-season snow cover and resort openings vary. Read [where it snows in December](/blog/does-it-snow-in-japan-in-december/) before adding a ski stop.
