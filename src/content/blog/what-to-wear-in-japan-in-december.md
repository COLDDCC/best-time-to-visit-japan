## December packing: choose your destination

| Destination | Typical December high / low | What to wear |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo | 12.0°C / 3.8°C | Warm coat, sweater, long trousers, closed walking shoes |
| Osaka | 12.3°C / 5.3°C | Warm coat and removable layers; scarf for colder evenings |
| Sapporo | 2.0°C / −4.0°C | Insulated coat, gloves, hat, warm layers and suitable winter footwear |
| Naha, Okinawa | 21.5°C / 16.8°C | Lighter layers and a jacket for wind or evenings |

*JMA 1991–2020 station normals. These are average daily highs and lows, not a forecast. Check Kyoto and other destinations separately.*

### Quick December packing checklist

- [ ] Warm outer coat for Tokyo, Kyoto or Osaka
- [ ] Long sleeves, sweater and long trousers
- [ ] Comfortable shoes suitable for the local forecast
- [ ] Scarf and optional gloves for outdoor evenings
- [ ] Waterproof winter footwear and insulating accessories for snow country
- [ ] Lightweight alternatives for Okinawa

## What to wear in Tokyo, Osaka and Kyoto

Staying mainly in Tokyo? The [Tokyo in December weather guide](/blog/tokyo-in-december-weather/) has a day-by-day outfit table and year-end checks.

| Part of your day | Outfit starting point | Keep accessible |
|---|---|---|
| Morning sightseeing | Long sleeves, sweater or fleece, warm outer coat | Scarf if needed |
| Walking between attractions | Long trousers and comfortable closed walking shoes | A removable layer |
| Heated train, café or shop | Remove the outer layer as needed | A bag for your coat or sweater |
| Evening illuminations | Warm coat and an extra layer if standing still | Scarf and gloves for the forecast |

Thermal underwear is a personal choice. Adjust warmth to the local forecast, your activity and how easily you feel cold.

Check Kyoto’s local forecast separately: Osaka’s figures provide context, not an exact substitute. For temple visits and outdoor gardens, think about long periods outside and grip on wet surfaces as well as the air temperature.

## What to wear in Hokkaido or snow country

For outdoor winter conditions, pack an insulating layer, a warm coat, hat, gloves and waterproof footwear suitable for the expected snow or slush. Warm socks help, but shoes also need enough room for your feet; overly tight layers are uncomfortable.

Check whether your itinerary includes maintained city streets, a resort or outdoor trails. Skiing and winter hiking need activity-specific equipment. A generic holiday packing list is not enough for exposed mountain travel.

Read [Does it snow in Japan in December?](/blog/does-it-snow-in-japan-in-december/) before choosing a destination around snow. Conditions and resort access vary during the month.

## Can you wear sneakers in December?

| Conditions on your route | Footwear starting point |
|---|---|
| Dry city walking | Comfortable closed shoes or sneakers, if warm enough for you |
| Wet city streets | Rain-tolerant footwear with grip |
| Snow, slush or ice | Waterproof, insulated winter footwear appropriate to the conditions |
| Skiing or winter hiking | Activity-specific equipment; check the operator or route requirements |

Check each destination before deciding to travel with only one pair.

Do not assume that normal city footwear is adequate for a snowy day trip. If a route requires winter equipment or is closed, change the plan rather than relying on shoes alone.

## Okinawa: a lighter December wardrobe

Naha’s December averages are much milder than those of Tokyo or Sapporo. Pack lighter layers with a jacket for cool or windy periods. Bring rain protection and check conditions for each island and activity.

If combining Okinawa and Hokkaido, pack distinct warm- and cold-weather options. One outfit selected from a supposed national average will not serve both well.

## Evenings, heated interiors and travel days

For outdoor illuminations, take an extra warm layer if you expect to stand still. Keep gloves accessible rather than buried in your suitcase. Inside trains and buildings, removable layers let you adjust without changing your whole outfit.

Recheck the forecast before every region change. A clear day in Tokyo says little about a mountain destination several hours away. For a trip beginning in late autumn, compare the [November clothing guide](/blog/what-to-wear-in-japan-in-november/).

## Sources and how to use these figures

Weather figures use [Japan Meteorological Agency climate normals for 1991–2020](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/stats/en/normal/normal.html). They describe long-term conditions at individual stations, not the forecast for your holiday. Precipitation includes rain and melted snow; monthly totals do not tell you how many days will be wet. Check the [JMA forecast](https://www.jma.go.jp/bosai/forecast/) near departure and again during your trip.
