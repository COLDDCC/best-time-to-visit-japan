**Choose November if your priority is milder city sightseeing and autumn gardens. Choose December if you prefer winter illuminations or a trip focused on northern or mountain snow destinations, while checking current snow conditions.** For Tokyo and Osaka, December is colder and has lower average precipitation. Neither month has one weather pattern across the whole country.

If your trip crosses both months, use our [late-November to early-December guide](/blog/japan-weather-late-november-early-december).

## November vs December in Japan: decide in 30 seconds

| If you care most about… | Better starting choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Comfortable walking in Tokyo or Osaka | **November** | Both cities have milder average temperatures |
| Lower monthly precipitation in Tokyo or Osaka | **December** | Tokyo: 57.9 vs 96.3 mm; Osaka: 55.5 vs 72.5 mm |
| Autumn leaves in central Japan | **November** | Generally a more suitable planning window; check local foliage reports |
| Winter illuminations | **December** | More winter-focused events; confirm specific dates |
| Snow-country trip | **December**, conditionally | Better seasonal fit, but snowfall and resort opening are not guaranteed |
| Okinawa warmth | **November** | Naha averages are warmer than in December |

**Our pick for a first-time city trip:** November for comfortable outdoor days; December for a colder winter atmosphere. For both months, choose destinations before relying on national averages.


## November or December: a quick comparison

| Your priority | November | December |
|---|---|---|
| Tokyo and Osaka walking days | Milder average temperatures | Colder mornings and evenings |
| Rain in Tokyo and Osaka | Lower precipitation than October | Lower precipitation than November |
| Autumn leaves | A useful starting month for central-Japan foliage plans | Some late color may remain; check local reports |
| Snow-focused travel | Northern and mountain conditions vary | More winter-oriented, but resort conditions still need checking |
| Evening lights | Some winter events already begin | A major seasonal attraction; verify event dates |
| Clothing | Removable layers and a jacket for many city routes | Warmer outerwear for city trips; winter equipment for snow country |

These are planning distinctions, not guarantees about your dates. A mountain resort, a coastal city and a southern island need separate weather checks.

## How much colder is December?

The following figures are monthly mean temperatures at individual JMA stations, not average daily highs or lows.

| Station | November mean | December mean |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tokyo | 12.5°C | 7.7°C |
| Osaka | 13.8°C | 8.7°C |
| Sapporo | 5.2°C | −0.9°C |
| Fukuoka | 14.2°C | 9.1°C |
| Niigata | 10.5°C | 5.3°C |
| Naha, Okinawa | 22.5°C | 19.0°C |

For daily high and low averages, see the [November weather page](/month/november) and [December weather page](/month/december). Kyoto needs a separate station record; Osaka is useful regional context but should not be substituted for Kyoto’s exact conditions.

## Which month is less rainy?

Tokyo’s precipitation normal falls from 96.3 mm in November to 57.9 mm in December. Osaka’s falls from 72.5 mm to 55.5 mm. On those routes, December is the drier month by monthly totals.

That conclusion does not apply everywhere: Niigata’s normal rises from 203.5 mm to 225.9 mm. Precipitation includes rain and melted snow, so these totals do not describe snowy ground conditions or the number of wet days. Read [Is Japan rainy in November?](/blog/is-japan-rainy-in-november) for the regional comparison.

## Which month is better for autumn leaves?

November is the more natural starting point for a central-Japan foliage itinerary. JNTO describes autumn colors in many places through November, with some viewing extending into early December. Timing depends on the location, elevation and year.

Plan around current local reports rather than a fixed national peak. A late-November Kyoto itinerary and a November Hokkaido itinerary are not equivalent foliage choices. Do not assume that waiting until December will produce the same colors with fewer visitors.

## Which month is better for snow?

December fits a winter-focused trip more naturally, especially in northern and mountain regions. However, early-season resort access and snow cover depend on the weather. Verify the resort’s actual open terrain, transport and cancellation terms before booking a ski-centered itinerary.

Tokyo and Osaka should be planned as cold city breaks, not guaranteed snow destinations. Our [December snow guide](/blog/does-it-snow-in-japan-in-december) explains the differences between city sightseeing and snow-country travel.

## What changes in your suitcase?

For November city trips, build around long sleeves, a removable sweater and jacket. In December, a warmer coat and accessories become more useful. Your destination and personal cold tolerance still matter more than a generic list.

Use the [November packing guide](/blog/what-to-wear-in-japan-in-november) or [December packing guide](/blog/what-to-wear-in-japan-in-december), especially when combining Okinawa with northern Japan.

## Is either month cheaper or quieter?

Do not assume either month is always cheaper. Compare accommodation for your exact dates and route. Foliage destinations, winter resorts, events and year-end travel can have different demand patterns. Near New Year, verify attraction opening hours and transport arrangements individually.

## How to choose

- Pick November for a trip centered on autumn gardens and milder urban walking.
- Pick December for winter city evenings or a condition-checked snow-country itinerary.
- For a trip spanning both, choose your essential destinations first and keep weather-sensitive day trips flexible.
