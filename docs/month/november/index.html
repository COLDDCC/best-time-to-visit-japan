Weather at a glance

What is Japan’s weather like in November?

November is cool in Tokyo and Osaka and relatively dry compared with September and October. Tokyo averages a daily high of 16.7°C and low of 8.8°C, with 96.3 mm of precipitation. It is not the main rainy season, but rain remains possible. Hokkaido is much colder, while Japan’s Sea of Japan coast can be considerably wetter.

    Why November Is Special

    Autumn color needs a local plan

    November is a popular time for autumn gardens in Kyoto, Nara and Tokyo. Timing varies with location, elevation and the weather that year: northern and high-elevation places can turn earlier. Check local foliage reports rather than assuming the same dates work everywhere.

    Plan outdoor visits around the weather

    Tokyo and Osaka are relatively dry compared with October, but rain remains possible and the Sea of Japan coast is wetter. Keep an indoor alternative for a wet day and warm layers for evening walks.

    Photography and sightseeing

    Check each garden’s actual opening hours and any booking rules. Popular foliage sites can be busy; allow extra time rather than relying on a fixed queue estimate.

    What to Pack

    For Tokyo and Osaka, bring long sleeves, a removable warm layer, a jacket and a compact umbrella. Add a winter coat and suitable footwear for Hokkaido or mountain routes; Okinawa needs lighter clothing.

    Where to Base Yourself in November

    Kyoto for color, Tokyo for value

    Kyoto is the koyo capital — Kiyomizu-dera, Eikando and the Philosopher's Path glow for three weeks. Base there for the leaves, or in Osaka for cheaper hotels with the same temples 30 minutes away. Tokyo's gardens and Nikko day trips make a fine color itinerary too.

    Best November Day Trips

    • Arashiyama & the bamboo grove — maples over the Katsura River
    • Nara — Todai-ji and the deer park in peak autumn light
    • Nikko — the final, most dramatic koyo of the main season

    Money in November

    November prices approach spring levels in Kyoto but stay moderate elsewhere. Early November (before the 15th) is cheaper than the peak color window — a smart trade for value travelers.

    Local Tip

    Check official opening times, reservation requirements and current foliage reports before choosing a temple visit. Evening illumination dates and admission arrangements vary by venue and year.
