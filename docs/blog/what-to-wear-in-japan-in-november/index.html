What to Wear in Japan in November: A Regional Packing Guide

Tokyo averages 7.4 rainy days in November: about 1.7 per 7-day trip (a climate estimate, not a forecast). Pack a compact umbrella and grippy walking shoes. For Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka, add long sleeves, a sweater and a removable jacket; northern routes need warmer, waterproof gear.

What shoes should you wear in Japan in November?

Your route Shoes to start with When to change the choice
Tokyo and Osaka city walks Broken-in walking shoes or sneakers with grip Add water resistance if wet days are forecast
Kyoto gardens and temple steps Comfortable shoes with a grippy sole, easy to remove Avoid smooth soles on wet stone
Hokkaido or a mountain extension Footwear selected for the local snow and ice forecast Use insulated waterproof winter footwear when wintry conditions require it

For a city itinerary, comfortable walking shoes are usually more practical than packing snow boots solely because it is November. Check your actual route before deciding.

Tokyo’s November climate averages are 16.7°C for the daily high and 8.8°C for the daily low. They are not an hourly forecast. Check the Tokyo November rain and outfit guide, particularly before a day trip outside the city.

For Tokyo and Osaka, removable layers also help on heated trains and indoors. Carry a small bag for the layer you take off. In Kyoto, choose shoes that are easy to remove at temples and grip wet stone steps.

Hokkaido and mountain routes: pack for colder conditions

Bring a warmer coat, gloves, a hat and warmer base layers. Waterproof shoes are useful for frequent wet days; choose insulated winter boots when snow or ice conditions require them. Check the actual destination and elevation before mountain trips—city clothing is not a hiking equipment list.

Okinawa: keep lighter options

Pack light tops, breathable trousers and a light jacket for wind or cooler evenings. Naha represents one station: check each island’s forecast and use the operator’s conditions for swimming or marine activities.

Early versus late November

Keep removable layers in early November, then reassess nighttime lows and outdoor evening plans later in the month. Sunset comes earlier as November progresses, so finish outdoor walks before dusk and keep a warm layer accessible; exact sunset time depends on your city and date.

Sources and how to use these figures

The city table links each measurement to its original Japan Meteorological Agency station page: main elements for temperature and precipitation details for wet-day counts. All figures use JMA 1991–2020 normals, verified on October 28, 2026. A rainy day means daily precipitation ≥1.0 mm, including melted snow; it does not mean rain all day.

The trip-length calculation scales monthly wet days by 7/30 or 14/30, rather than predicting the weather on specific dates. The planner uses the unrounded weekly rate: up to 2 wet days suggests an umbrella and grippy shoes; over 2 through 3 adds a waterproof shell and water-resistant shoes; over 3, or a Sea of Japan coast route, suggests a waterproof jacket and waterproof shoes. These are packing rules, not JMA recommendations. For rain patterns and indoor alternatives, see rainy days by city. Check the JMA forecast near departure.

    Quick questions

    Can I wear sneakers in Japan in November?

    For city sightseeing, broken-in sneakers or walking shoes with grip are a practical starting point. Check the rain forecast; snowy northern or mountain routes may need winter footwear.

    Do I need a winter coat in Tokyo in November?

    Start with long sleeves, a removable sweater and a jacket. Choose a warmer coat if you feel cold easily or plan long evenings outdoors; use the local forecast before packing.

    What should be on my November packing list?

    Long sleeves, trousers, a sweater or fleece, a jacket, comfortable shoes with grip and compact rain protection. Add warmer layers and suitable footwear for northern or mountain destinations.

    Will it rain during my November trip to Japan?

    Tokyo averages 7.4 days with at least 1 mm of precipitation in November. Scaling that monthly normal gives about 1.7 wet days over 7 days, or 3.5 over 14 days. This is a historical planning estimate, not a forecast; wet spells and dry trips are both possible.

    Do I need waterproof shoes in Japan in November?

    For Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka, grippy walking shoes and a compact umbrella are a practical starting point; waterproof boots are usually unnecessary for city sightseeing alone. Sapporo averages 14.6 wet days and Niigata 17.7, so waterproof footwear is more useful there. Choose winter boots only if local snow or ice conditions require them.
