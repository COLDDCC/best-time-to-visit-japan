Christmas & New Year in Japan: Which Dates to Choose?

Choose a December city break for winter lights; choose New Year if shrine visits are a priority and you can plan around busy travel and holiday closures. Check each venue's opening dates before fixing your route.

JNTO’s December guide describes the New Year period as busy and expensive, with some businesses closing for a few days. It gives December 29 to January 3 as a typical holiday window; individual schedules vary. This page is a planning guide, not a confirmed event calendar for 2026–27.

Before Christmas: plan around the lights you want

Winter illuminations are a reason to consider a December city break. Some begin in November; dates differ by event. Choose the specific display first, then check admission, opening evenings and transport on the organizer’s website. Do not assume that every display ends on Christmas Day.

December 29 to early January: make a closure plan

Check What to record
Must-see attraction Its exact open dates and last admission
Restaurant Holiday schedule and reservation confirmation
Intercity transport Available departures and reservation requirements
Local return journey Last service or confirmed holiday timetable
Snow-country extension Weather, open terrain and access report

Keep one confirmed alternative for each day. A business closing does not mean an entire city closes, but it can change a carefully timed itinerary.

Does Christmas mean snow in Tokyo?

A Tokyo or Osaka trip should not depend on snowfall. Northern and mountain destinations offer a different winter experience, but early-season snow cover and resort openings vary. Read where it snows in December before adding a ski stop.

Ready-to-use planning checklist

  • Pick the experience: lights, shrine visits or snow
  • Confirm opening dates directly with each must-see venue
  • Check transport and accommodation for the exact holiday dates
  • Keep one confirmed backup per day
  • Use the destination forecast before finalizing clothing
  • Compare January if your trip crosses into the new year

Quick questions

Is everything closed in Japan over New Year?

No, but some businesses close for several days. Opening dates vary by venue. Check every must-see attraction, restaurant and transport operator rather than applying one nationwide schedule.

Will Tokyo be snowy at Christmas?

Do not count on snow for a Tokyo city break. Northern and mountain destinations need separate snow and access checks, especially early in the ski season.
