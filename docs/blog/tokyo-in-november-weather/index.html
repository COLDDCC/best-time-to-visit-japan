Tokyo in November: Rain, Weather & What to Wear

Tokyo in November is cool rather than reliably warm: average daily highs are 16.7°C and lows 8.8°C. Rain is possible, so start with removable layers, walking shoes with grip and a compact umbrella.

Early or late November?

JNTO’s November guide describes increasingly chilly mornings and evenings, with autumn color varying by place. Use a monthly average to shortlist clothing, then check daily highs and lows near departure. Do not treat an early-November afternoon and a late-November evening as the same packing problem.

For trips continuing into winter, compare late November and early December.

A rain backup you can use

  • Carry a compact umbrella or rain shell
  • Keep one indoor attraction available for a wet afternoon
  • Check its opening day and reservation rules
  • Wear shoes with grip for wet paving and steps
  • Check the JMA forecast near departure and during the trip

Quick questions

Does it rain a lot in Tokyo in November?

Tokyo's November precipitation normal is 96.3 mm, lower than October's 234.8 mm. This is a monthly total, not a rainy-day count or a guarantee that your dates will be dry.

What should I wear in Tokyo in November?

Start with long sleeves, trousers, a removable sweater and a jacket. Add a scarf for cool evenings if needed, and take a compact umbrella and comfortable shoes with grip.

Can I use Tokyo's weather forecast for a mountain day trip?

No. Check the actual destination, elevation and route conditions. A city forecast does not describe the higher ground around Hakone or other mountain routes.
