Tokyo Weather in November: Rain, Sunshine & Daylight

Tokyo in November is cool rather than reliably warm: average daily highs are 16.7°C and lows 8.8°C. Rain is possible, so start with removable layers, walking shoes with grip and a compact umbrella.

    Sunshine and sunset are different measurements

    Tokyo’s JMA normal is 149.8 hours of sunshine during November, about five hours per day averaged across the month. This measures bright sunshine, not the length of daylight, and does not promise five sunny hours on each travel day.

    The National Astronomical Observatory of Japan’s Tokyo calendar for November 2026 lists sunset at 16:35 JST on November 14. Use its daily table for your date; allow time to finish outdoor sightseeing before dusk and check each garden’s actual closing time. Sunset time is not an admission deadline.

    Quick questions

    Does it rain a lot in Tokyo in November?

    Tokyo averages 7.4 days with at least 1 mm of precipitation in November, equivalent to about 1.7 wet days over seven days at the monthly average rate. These climate normals do not predict your dates.

    What should I wear in Tokyo in November?

    Start with long sleeves, trousers, a removable sweater and a jacket. Add a scarf for cool evenings if needed, and take a compact umbrella and comfortable shoes with grip.

    Can I use Tokyo's weather forecast for a mountain day trip?

    No. Check the actual destination, elevation and route conditions. A city forecast does not describe the higher ground around Hakone or other mountain routes.
