Is Japan Rainy in November? Rainfall by City & What to Expect

Mostly no. November is not Japan's rainy season, and Tokyo (96.3 mm) and Osaka (72.5 mm) are far drier than in October. The exception is the Sea of Japan coast: Niigata averages 203.5 mm. Pack a compact umbrella for city trips and proper rain gear if your route crosses to the Sea of Japan side.

    Is November Japan’s rainy season?

    November is outside the main early-summer rainy season, usually called tsuyu. That does not mean rain stops: weather systems can still bring wet days, while northern and mountain destinations may experience snow. JNTO’s November guide describes generally crisp air and clear skies across much of Japan, alongside increasingly chilly mornings and evenings.

    Which Japanese cities are best for avoiding November rain?

    If minimizing precipitation is your main priority, the historical station totals in the table favor Osaka (72.5 mm) and Tokyo (96.3 mm) over Niigata (203.5 mm). This is a comparison of monthly precipitation, not a ranking of the probability of rainy days or a guarantee of sunshine. Sapporo’s total can include snow.

    For a first-time Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka itinerary, keep outdoor attractions flexible and use the local forecast as your dates approach. Use Kyoto’s own row for that stop rather than substituting Osaka’s measurements.

    Is November wetter than October or December?

    For Tokyo and Osaka, November is drier than October by monthly precipitation, and December is drier again. Tokyo also averages more sunshine in November (149.8 hours) than in October (129.4 hours). Niigata follows a different pattern: its precipitation increases toward winter. That difference matters if your itinerary crosses from Tokyo to Japan’s Sea of Japan coast.

    A lower monthly total does not guarantee that a particular seven-day visit will stay dry. You also cannot turn 96.3 mm into a personal probability of rain without information about daily rainfall and its distribution.

    Does it rain a lot in Tokyo or Kyoto in November?

    Tokyo’s November normal is 96.3 mm, compared with 234.8 mm in October. That supports describing November as relatively dry, rather than rain-free. Keep one indoor option available for a wet afternoon.

    Kyoto’s own JMA station averages 73.9 mm and 6.3 days with at least 1 mm in November. Kyoto’s temple walks also involve outdoor paths and steps, so wet-weather footwear matters even on an otherwise city-focused trip.

    Quick questions

    Will it rain every day? Monthly averages cannot answer that for your dates. November is not the main rainy season, but a wet spell is possible.

    Do I need waterproof shoes? Comfortable shoes that tolerate some rain are helpful in cities. Choose more protective footwear for snow, slush or outdoor routes.

    Is December a better choice to avoid rain? Tokyo and Osaka have lower December precipitation normals, but it is colder. Compare the December weather guide before choosing solely on rainfall.

    Sources and how to use these figures

    Weather figures use Japan Meteorological Agency climate normals for 1991–2020. They describe long-term conditions at individual stations, not the forecast for your holiday. Precipitation includes rain and melted snow; monthly totals do not tell you how many days will be wet. Check the JMA forecast near departure and again during your trip.

    Quick questions

    Is November the rainy season in Japan?

    No. Japan's main rainy season (tsuyu) is in early summer. November is relatively dry in Tokyo (96.3 mm) and Osaka (72.5 mm) compared with October, although rain is still possible and the Sea of Japan coast is much wetter (Niigata: 203.5 mm).

    Does it rain a lot in Japan in November?

    Not in the main tourist cities. Tokyo's November precipitation normal is 96.3 mm, less than half of October's 234.8 mm, and Osaka's is 72.5 mm. Niigata on the Sea of Japan coast averages 203.5 mm, so a route there needs more rain gear. These are monthly totals from JMA 1991–2020 normals, not a forecast.

    Is November or December drier in Japan?

    In Tokyo and Osaka, December is drier by monthly totals (Tokyo 57.9 mm, Osaka 55.5 mm) but noticeably colder. On the Sea of Japan coast the pattern reverses: Niigata gets wetter from November (203.5 mm) to December (225.9 mm).

    Does it snow in Japan in November?

    Snow in November is mainly a concern for Hokkaido, northern Japan and mountain areas. Sapporo's average November low is 1.6°C, so some precipitation there can fall as snow. Snow is not something to plan around in Tokyo or Osaka in November.

    Is November sunny in Tokyo?

    Tokyo's November sunshine normal is 149.8 hours, more than October's 129.4 hours, which fits the drier, clearer autumn pattern. Individual days can still be cloudy or wet.
