Kyoto in November: Weather, Autumn Leaves & Planning

Kyoto in November suits autumn gardens and cool city walks. Plan removable layers for chilly mornings and evenings, shoes with grip for temple paths, and a flexible indoor alternative. Check official foliage reports for each place rather than booking around a predicted peak date.

    Kyoto weather and the morning-to-evening difference

    The average daily high and low in the station table describe typical daytime and overnight conditions across the full month. They are not temperatures you will necessarily encounter on one day. Carry a removable sweater and jacket so you can adjust between outdoor walks, transport and heated interiors.

    For an evening outdoors, check the hourly forecast and bring the warmer layer with you. A mountain excursion needs its own forecast and route conditions; the Kyoto urban station is not a mountain weather report.

    Autumn leaves: choose places with current reports

    Use Kyoto City’s official foliage reports to compare individual locations. Check the report date and year: an older calendar or photograph does not establish current conditions. Higher ground and city gardens can be at different stages on the same day.

    Keep the order of garden visits flexible within your stay. Confirm current opening hours, reservation requirements and any evening admission arrangements on the venue’s own website. This guide does not predict a peak date or queue duration.

    Temple paths, shoes and socks

    Choose broken-in walking shoes with grip for wet stone, steps and garden paths. Some indoor temple areas ask visitors to remove shoes; follow the posted instructions and wear clean socks. Easy-to-remove footwear can make repeated transitions simpler, but walking comfort comes first.

    A compact umbrella is a practical city starting point. Waterproof footwear is a route and forecast decision rather than a requirement for every Kyoto November visit. See what to wear in Japan in November for the city decision table and editable packing planner.

    Crowds and choosing a base

    Check the Japan crowd calendar for holiday context and verify local event dates. Compare accommodation for your exact stay. Choose Kyoto or Osaka based on the places you plan to visit, transport connections and availability; neither a fixed hotel saving nor a fixed temple queue is assumed here.

    For trips continuing into winter, compare late November and early December. For a different city, use Tokyo weather in November.

    Sources and limits

    All measurements in the shared station table link directly to the JMA station normals. Foliage planning uses Kyoto City’s official reports. Climate averages help with preparation; use the JMA forecast for your actual travel dates.

    Quick questions

    Is November a good time to visit Kyoto?

    November is a useful starting point for an autumn-garden trip. Color depends on location, elevation and the year's weather. Check official local reports and allow for crowds at famous sites.

    Can I use Osaka weather data for Kyoto?

    Use Kyoto's own JMA station normals and local forecast. Nearby Osaka provides regional context but does not represent Kyoto's exact temperatures or rainfall.

    What shoes should I wear for Kyoto temples in November?

    Choose comfortable walking shoes with grip for wet paving and steps. Shoes that are easy to remove and clean socks help where indoor areas require shoes to be removed; follow each venue's instructions.
