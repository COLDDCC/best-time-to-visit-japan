Best Time to Visit Kyoto and Osaka: Sakura, Koyo (2026)

Best time to visit Kyoto: late March-early April for cherry blossoms, mid-late November for autumn leaves. Osaka shines in the same windows, both crowded.

The Short Answer

Overview

Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart, and its two iconic seasons are sakura (late March-early April) and autumn leaves (mid-late November). Both are breathtaking and crowded. Fushimi Inari's thousand torii gates and the golden light of Kinkaku-ji shine in every season. Nearby Osaka — Japan's kitchen — delivers Dotonbori street food, Osaka Castle, and Universal Studios year-round, and Nara's bowing deer are a short train ride away. For a balanced, less manic visit, late May, October, and February are excellent quieter alternatives.

Weather by Season

Spring (Mar-May)

10-22°C

Sakura in early April. Pleasant, but the single busiest window of the year.

Summer (Jun-Aug)

25-34°C

Hot and humid, with tsuyu rain early in June. The Gion Matsuri (July) is the festival highlight.

Autumn (Sep-Nov)

8-20°C

Peak koyo in mid-late November. Crisp air, brilliant temples, big crowds.

Winter (Dec-Feb)

1-10°C

Cold, dry, and quiet. Famous spots are far emptier — great for photography.

Must-Do Experiences

  • See Kinkakuji and Kiyomizu-dera — arrive before 8 AM to beat the tour buses.
  • Walk the thousands of vermilion torii at Fushimi Inari.
  • Eat your way through Dotonbori in Osaka — takoyaki, okonomiyaki, glowing signs.
  • Day trip to Nara to feed the bowing deer and see Todai-ji's Great Buddha.

Festivals & Highlights

  • Gion Matsuri, Kyoto — July, one of Japan's three great festivals.
  • Autumn foliage at Kiyomizu-dera and Kinkakuji — mid-late November.
  • Tenjin Matsuri, Osaka — late July, a massive river fireworks festival.

Local Tip

Kyoto's famous sights are packed year-round. Start before 8 AM for the 'golden hour' and prebook what you can — it makes an enormous difference.

Kyoto & Osaka FAQs

How crowded is Kyoto in sakura season?

Extremely. Expect long queues at top temples and packed streets, especially weekends. Go early each day and book ahead where possible.

Is Kyoto expensive?

Comparable to Tokyo: hotels $60-130/night, meals $6-40. Osaka is slightly cheaper, and street food keeps costs down.

How many days do I need in Kyoto?

3 days covers the top sites. Add a day each for Nara and Osaka, or base yourself in Osaka to save money.
