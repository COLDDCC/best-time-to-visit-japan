Best Time to Visit Hokkaido: Snow & Summer (2026)

Best time to visit Hokkaido: summer (June-August) for cool hiking and flowers, or winter (January-February) for skiing & the Sapporo Snow Festival.

The Short Answer

The best time to visit Hokkaido is June-August & January-February, when temperatures are Summer 15-25°C and the weather is at its most comfortable.

Plan around November (grey & snowy, no festivals).

Best Months

June-August & January-February

Worst Months

November (grey & snowy, no festivals)

Ideal Conditions

Jun-Aug or Jan-Feb · Summer 15-25°C / Winter -10 to 0°C

Daily Cost

$40-120 hotels / $4-30 meals / day

Overview

Hokkaido is Japan's northernmost main island and its least humid corner. Winters are harsh but legendary — the Sapporo Snow Festival and Niseko's light, dry powder draw skiers from around the world. Summers are gloriously mild, topping out around 25°C with long daylight hours. Spring arrives two to four weeks later than Tokyo, and autumn colors start earlier. It is the only place in Japan where you can ski powder in winter and hike cool green trails in July without the humidity that blankets the rest of the country.

Weather by Season

Spring (May-Jun)

8-18°C

Late bloomers: sakura peaks in May. Tulip and pink-moss fields carpet Biei. Rain is light.

Summer (Jul-Aug)

18-26°C

Cool, green, and perfect for hiking. Furano's lavender fields bloom in July. The freshest escape from mainland heat.

Autumn (Sep-Nov)

5-15°C

Crisp, dry air. Daisetsuzan shows Japan's first autumn colors in September. November turns grey and snowy.

Winter (Dec-Mar)

-10 to 0°C

Heavy powder snow. Skiing at Niseko and the Sapporo Snow Festival (early Feb) are the winter highlights.

Must-Do Experiences

  • Ski or snowboard Niseko's famously light powder in winter.
  • See the Sapporo Snow Festival's giant illuminated ice sculptures (early February).
  • Walk the lavender fields of Farm Tomita in Furano (July).
  • Hike in Daisetsuzan National Park — Japan's largest — in the cool green summer.

Festivals & Highlights

  • Sapporo Snow Festival — early February.
  • Furano & Biei flower season — July.
  • Daisetsuzan autumn colors — late September to mid-October.

Local Tip

Hokkaido has two completely different versions: summer for cool hiking and flowers, winter for the Snow Festival and world-class powder. Pick your season before you book.

Hokkaido FAQs

How cold does Hokkaido get in winter?

Sapporo averages -3 to 0°C in January, but interior towns like Asahikawa can drop below -10°C. Powder snow accumulates deep, so bring waterproof boots and serious layers.

Is Hokkaido good in summer?

Excellent. Temperatures hover between 18-26°C with low humidity, making it an ideal escape from mainland heat. July-August is also the lavender and flower peak.

How many days do I need in Hokkaido?

5-7 days covers Sapporo, Otaru, and a ski or hiking base. Give yourself 10-14 days if you also want Furano, Biei, and Daisetsuzan in summer.
