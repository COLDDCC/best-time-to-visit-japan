Best Time to Visit Nara: Deer, Temples & Leaves (2026)

Best time to visit Nara: late March-April for sakura, October-November for autumn leaves. The deer park and Great Buddha work year-round as an easy day trip.

The Short Answer

The best time to visit Nara is March-April & October-November, when temperatures are Spring 10-22°C and the weather is at its most comfortable.

Plan around July-August (hot & rainy).

Overview

Nara was Japan's first permanent capital, and it preserves 8th-century treasures — chief among them Todai-ji, the world's largest wooden building, housing a colossal bronze Buddha. The park's famous semi-wild deer bow to visitors for deer crackers. Nara shines brightest in cherry-blossom spring and autumn-color fall, but the Great Buddha and quiet old town reward visitors in any season. It's compact enough to explore in a day, making it a perfect pairing with Kyoto or Osaka.

Weather by Season

10-22°C

Sakura in Nara Park and around the temples is picture-perfect, with crowds to match.

Summer (Jun-Aug)

24-33°C

Hot and humid with tsuyu rain in June. Deer hide in the shade at midday.

Autumn (Oct-Nov)

8-20°C

Autumn leaves in the hills and temple gardens peak in November. Gorgeous.

Winter (Dec-Feb)

1-11°C

Cold and quiet. An empty, atmospheric beauty few travelers see.

Must-Do Experiences

  • Meet Nara Park's deer — they bow politely for deer crackers.
  • Enter Todai-ji and stand under the Great Buddha.
  • Walk Kasuga Taisha's forest of thousands of stone and bronze lanterns.
  • See Nara's old machiya townhouses and tiny museums near the station.

Festivals & Highlights

  • Shuni-e (Omizutori) fire festival at Todai-ji — early March.
  • Kasuga Wakamiya On-matsuri — December, a grand historical procession.
  • Nara Light Festival and autumn leaves — October-November.

Local Tip

Nara's free-roaming deer bow for crackers, and Todai-ji's Great Buddha is one of the world's largest bronze statues. An easy day trip from Kyoto or Osaka.

Nara FAQs

How do I get to Nara from Kyoto or Osaka?

About 45-60 minutes by train from either city. It's perfectly feasible as a day trip, though staying overnight lets you enjoy the park at dawn.

Are the deer dangerous?

No, but they'll nibble if they think you have crackers. Buy the special deer senbei and feed them one at a time at a safe distance.

What's the best season for the deer park?

Autumn is the biggest draw — maple leaves frame the park and temples. Winter offers far fewer people.
