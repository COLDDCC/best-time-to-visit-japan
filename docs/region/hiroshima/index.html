Best Time to Visit Hiroshima & Miyajima (2026)

Best time to visit Hiroshima: spring (March-May) and autumn (October-November). Miyajima's floating torii and the Peace Memorial shine in mild, clear weather.

Overview

Hiroshima is a city of profound history — the Atomic Bomb Dome and Peace Memorial Park are its emotional heart, and every traveler should visit. A short ferry ride away, Miyajima Island's famous red floating torii gate hovers over the water at high tide, with Mt Misen rising behind it. The best weather is mild spring (March-May) and clear autumn (October-November). Midsummer is humid and typhoon-prone along the Inland Sea. Onsen towns and the Setouchi islands make the region a rewarding stop on any western Japan itinerary.

Spring (Mar-May)

12-22°C

Mild with sakura at the Peace Memorial Park and Shukkeien garden.

Summer (Jun-Aug)

23-33°C

Hot and humid; typhoons begin to threaten by late July. Watch the forecast on the Inland Sea.

Autumn (Oct-Nov)

12-22°C

Clear, crisp and comfortable — the best window. Autumn leaves around the castle and coastal temples.

Winter (Dec-Feb)

1-10°C

Cold but dry and quiet. Miyajima is peaceful and onsen towns are cozy.

Must-Do Experiences

  • Tour the Peace Memorial Park and museum — a humbling, essential day.
  • Take the ferry to Miyajima and photograph the floating gate at high tide.
  • Climb Mt Misen on Miyajima for sweeping views of the Seto Inland Sea.
  • Eat Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki, layered and griddled at the table.

Festivals & Highlights

  • Peace Memorial Ceremony — August 6.
  • Kanda & coastal summer matsuri along the Inland Sea — July-August.
  • Autumn illumination at Hiroshima Castle — November.

Local Tip

Miyajima's floating torii is best seen at high tide — it rises and falls twice a day, so time your ferry accordingly.

Hiroshima FAQs

When should I avoid typhoons in Hiroshima?

Typhoon season concentrates in July-September along the Inland Sea. Spring and October-November are the most reliable windows.

How long should I spend in Hiroshima?

Two days is ideal: one for the city and Peace Museum, one for Miyajima. Time the torii's high tide around mid-morning or late afternoon.

Is Miyajima worth the trip?

Absolutely — the floating torii and Mt Misen views are iconic, and the island's free-roaming deer make it a highlight of western Japan.
