How to read

Read it in
three colours

This calendar estimates crowd levels for the major travel regions of Japan — Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Nara, and Hakone combined — for every day of the year.

Red means queues of 1–2 hours at the famous sites and packed streets. Green means you can often have a landmark almost to yourself. Most of the year sits quietly in between.

Avoid

Sakura peak, Golden Week & mid-August Obon

The Sweet Spots

Early March · Late June · All of September · Early December

The Trade-off

Quieter months often come with rain, heat, or less iconic scenery
