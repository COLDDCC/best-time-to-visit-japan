Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What We Collect

When to Visit Japan does not require user accounts, collect personal information, or use cookies for tracking. We do not use analytics services that collect personal data. The quiz on our site stores your answers only in your browser session — they are never sent to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts to serve the Geist typeface. Google may collect standard server log information when loading fonts. We do not use any other third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services.

3. Contact Forms

If you contact us via email, we will use your email address solely to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to mailing lists, share your email, or use it for any purpose other than direct correspondence.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request information about any personal data we may hold about you, and to request its deletion. Since we collect essentially no personal data, this is straightforward — but we take it seriously nonetheless.

5. Changes to This Policy

We will update this page if our data practices change. The last updated date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Questions?

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at hello@when-to-visit-japan.com.
