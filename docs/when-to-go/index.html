Behind the Quiz

How We Calculate Your Result

This quiz isn't random. Each answer you give is weighted against three data dimensions: weather suitability (monthly climate patterns from the Japan Meteorological Agency), crowd avoidance (Japanese national holiday calendar including Golden Week and Obon), and cost optimization (seasonal flight and hotel price trends). Your preferences for temperature, crowds, and budget determine which months score highest.

Weather Match

Your temperature preference is matched against monthly climate normals for each region, weighted by your destination choice.

Crowd Filter

Your crowd tolerance is cross-referenced with Japan's holiday calendar (Golden Week, Obon, sakura peaks) and attraction visitor data.

Cost Adjustment

Your budget determines whether we recommend peak-season windows or off-season sweet spots like June and September.
