Hokkaido

When is the best time to visit Hokkaido?

Best

June-August & January-February

Avoid

November (grey & snowy, no festivals)

Ideal

Jun-Aug or Jan-Feb * Summer 15-25°C / Winter -10 to 0°C

Must-See

Sapporo, Niseko, Furano, Daisetsuzan

Cost

$40-120 hotels / $4-30 meals / day

Local Tip

Hokkaido has two completely different versions: summer for cool hiking and flowers, winter for the Snow Festival and world-class powder. Pick your season before you book.

Tohoku

When is the best time to visit Tohoku?

Best

August (Nebuta) & Feb (snow monsters)

Avoid

January & heavy-snow weeks

Ideal

May-Aug & Dec-Feb * Summer 18-28°C / Winter -5 to 5°C

Must-See

Aomori Nebuta, Sendai, Zao Onsen, Matsushima Bay

Cost

$35-80 hotels / $3-20 meals / day

Local Tip

Tohoku is Japan's best-value region: spectacular festivals, quiet temples, and friendly towns at a fraction of the Kyoto price. Don't skip the Nebuta Matsuri in August.

Tokyo

When is the best time to visit Tokyo?

Best

Late March-April & October-November

Avoid

July-August (humid) & Dec 28-Jan 3

Ideal

Mar-Apr & Oct-Nov * Spring 10-20°C / Autumn 12-22°C

Must-See

Shibuya, Asakusa, Hakone, Mount Fuji day trips

Cost

$60-150 hotels / $5-35 meals / day

Local Tip

Tokyo works year-round. Cherry blossoms (early April) and autumn leaves (late November) add magic, but even humid summers fill with fireworks festivals.

Mount Fuji & Chubu

When is the best time to visit Mount Fuji and the Chubu region?

Best

Clear views Oct-Apr / Climbing Jul-Sep

Avoid

June & early October (cloudy & rainy)

Ideal

Mar-Apr & Oct-Nov * Base 15-30°C / summit 5-15°C in season

Must-See

Mount Fuji, Hakone, Kanazawa, Takayama, Shirakawa-go

Cost

$40-90 hotels / $4-15 meals / day

Local Tip

The iconic silhouette of Fuji is clearest in winter and early spring. Summer clouds hide its summit for much of the day.

When is the best time to visit Kyoto and Osaka?

Best

Late March-April & November

Avoid

July-August (hot & humid)

Ideal

Mar-Apr & Nov * Spring 10-22°C / Autumn 8-18°C

Regional travel rules of thumb

Book Early for Sakura & Koyo

For cherry blossom season (late March-April) and autumn leaves (November), book flights and hotels 2-3 months ahead. Prices surge 40-80% during these weeks.

Avoid Golden Week & Obon

Golden Week (late April-May 5) and Obon (mid-August) are Japan's two biggest travel peaks. Every bullet train and hotel is packed. Travel just after and watch prices collapse.

Mix North & South

A 2-week trip can chase the seasons: Hokkaido in winter for snow, then Kyoto in late March for sakura, or Okinawa in spring then the Alps in autumn.
