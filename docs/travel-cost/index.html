05 &middot; Spend Less

1

Fly into Tokyo and out of Osaka (or vice versa) to save money and avoid backtracking.

2

Travel in June or September: flights and hotels drop 20-30% versus sakura and koyo peaks.

3

Use a 7-day JR Pass for long-haul shinkansen trips; ride local metros for anything under 30 minutes.

4

Eat where locals eat: ramen counters, curry shops, and conveyor sushi cost $8-15 and are outstanding.

5

Book hotels via Japanese platforms (Rakuten Travel, Japanican) for lower prices than Booking.com.

6

Avoid Golden Week (Apr 29-May 5), Obon (Aug 11-16), and New Year. Prices triple and rooms vanish.

7

Convenience stores (7-Eleven, FamilyMart) are your budget hero: great breakfasts for under $5.

8

Carry cash - many small shops and temple stalls in Japan are cash-only.
